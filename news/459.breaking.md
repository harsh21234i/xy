Chromium-backed image exports now fail if Chromium cannot start with its sandbox enabled;
they no longer retry without the sandbox. For trusted HTML in environments that cannot
start the sandbox, pass `sandbox=False` explicitly. Dependency audits now share one local
and CI entry point, recognize conventional lockfiles across supported ecosystems, and keep
historical benchmark locks visible under an explicit non-executable evidence exemption.
Repository-owned workflow benchmarks explicitly opt out only for their controlled HTML,
and browser benchmark jobs allow hosted-runner setup enough time to finish.
